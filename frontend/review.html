Why no database?

A fixed month does not need a queue, broker or database. Immutable JSON makes the result reviewable and keeps deployment small. Bounded in-memory caches avoid repeated calculations.

Where ingestion belongs

Outside the request path. Rebuild the snapshot on a network-connected machine, check coverage, then restart or redeploy. Web requests never mutate data or call Elexon.

02 · Temporal correctness

The publication timestamp is part of the forecast.

eligible = [
    row for row in versions_for_target

# Different targets may have different publication vintages.

Two deliberately different actual policies

Retrospective score: latest actual revision in the fetched snapshot, never described as settlement-final.

Decision-time error context: only earlier January decisions, completed intervals and actual publications available by the current cutoff minus buffer.

Time conventions

UTC internally. Europe/London for the decision calendar. January is GMT. Delivery-day slots respect 46 / 48 / 50 half-hours at clock changes, though this pilot uses 48.

publishTime is an availability proxy, not proof of receipt by the utility. The optional buffer is a sensitivity assumption, not measured transport latency.
03 · Analysis contract

Useful statistics. Visible limits.

MeasureDefinition on matched intervals
ErrorForecast − actual; positive means wind underdelivery.
MAE / RMSEMean absolute error / root mean squared error, in MW.
BiasMean signed error, in MW.
WAPE100 × sum |error| / sum |actual|. Null at zero denominator.
EnergyMW × 0.5 hours. Gaps mean a partial total, not a full day.

Exploratory prior-only range

Add the 10th and 90th percentiles of earlier actual-minus-forecast residuals to the current forecast. Require at least seven earlier decision days and 168 matched half-hours.

These are descriptive thresholds, not evidence of calibrated 80% coverage. Adjacent half-hours are correlated. There is no out-of-sample validation or monetary optimisation.

Portfolio boundary

National wind error is not the utility’s procurement requirement. Demand, contracted entitlement and existing positions are absent by design.
